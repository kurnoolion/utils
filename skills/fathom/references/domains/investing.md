# Domain pack — trading, investing, corporate events

## Primary sources (decide)
- SEC EDGAR: 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K (item numbers matter), S-1, 13F, Form 4, proxy statements; equivalents abroad (SEDAR, Companies House, EDINET).
- Earnings transcripts and investor presentations; guidance language verbatim.
- Regulators and courts: FTC/DOJ filings and HSR timelines, PACER dockets, sector regulators' decision calendars.
- Macro data: FRED, BLS, Census, central-bank statements and dot plots.
- Market-implied views: options-implied moves, prediction-market prices (a crowd forecast, not truth), short interest.

## Secondary (frame, not decide)
Bloomberg, Reuters, WSJ, FT; sell-side notes (always note the house's banking relationship); Seeking Alpha and forums as discovery only.

## Expert vocabulary for queries
Guidance, consensus, beat/miss, free cash flow, capex intensity, ARPU and churn, TAM, comps and multiples, catalysts, lock-up expiry, ex-dividend, index rebalance, covenant, going-concern language, non-GAAP reconciliation.

## Signpost calendar
- Earnings dates (company IR calendar); guidance revisions; 10-K filing deadlines (60–90 days after FY end).
- Regulatory decision deadlines; merger close dates and outside dates; lock-up expiries; index rebalances.
- Central-bank meetings (FOMC eight per year) and data releases (jobs, CPI).

## Pitfalls specific to this domain
- Say whether the call is about the **event** or the **price**. The price already contains public information; an edge exists only in synthesis or in something the market misweights.
- Narratives are written after price moves. Check the date order.
- Non-GAAP and "record" claims: reconcile to GAAP; check the base period.
- Survivorship in back-tests and case studies.
- Insiders and sell-side have incentives; tag accordingly. Nothing here is investment advice; state that when a reader might act.
