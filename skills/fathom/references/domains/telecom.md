# Domain pack — telecom, spectrum, devices-on-networks

## Primary sources (decide)
- **3GPP**: specs via the 3GPP portal; TDocs and meeting reports for who contributed what; plenary (TSG RAN/SA/CT) reports for decisions; release freeze dates.
- **Regulators**: FCC (ECFS dockets, EDOCS, auction pages, OET equipment authorization / FCC ID test reports), NTIA, Ofcom, BNetzA, ARCEP, ITU-R (WRC agenda items, recommendations).
- **Industry bodies**: GSMA (device database, intelligence), CTIA, ATIS Next G Alliance, O-RAN Alliance, AI-RAN Alliance, NGMN.
- **Company records**: carrier and vendor earnings transcripts, 10-K/10-Q, investor decks, spectrum holdings tables, press releases (dated).
- **Device truth**: FCC ID test reports (bands, power classes), regulator equipment databases, GSMA TAC records, OEM spec pages per region SKU, UE capability logs.

## Secondary (frame, not decide)
Light Reading, Fierce Network, SDxCentral, RCR Wireless, Mobile World Live, Telecompetitor, PolicyTracker (spectrum policy), analyst notes (Dell'Oro, Omdia — check who commissioned).

## Expert vocabulary for queries
NR band numbers (n77, n258…), FR1/FR2/FR3, SA vs NSA, carrier aggregation, DSS, RedCap, NTN and D2D, ISAC, L4S, VoNR, network slicing, PEA/CMA licence areas, build-out deadlines, upper mid-band, C-band, CBRS, Rel-18/19/20 work items, UE capability signalling, HPUE.

## Signpost calendar
- 3GPP plenaries: quarterly (Mar, Jun, Sep, Dec); release freeze milestones are decided there.
- FCC open meetings: monthly; auction start dates and comment cycles on the auction page.
- Carrier earnings: late Jan / Apr / Jul / Oct; spectrum commentary lives in the Q&A.
- MWC Barcelona: late Feb–early Mar (announcements cluster here); WRC every four years (WRC-27).
- Device launches: regulator filings appear 1–8 weeks before a launch; certification listings earlier.

## Pitfalls specific to this domain
- "Supports band X" (silicon) ≠ "enabled on this region SKU" ≠ "carrier has deployed X". Say which.
- Trial ≠ pre-commercial ≠ commercial. Date each.
- A standards freeze precedes device availability by roughly 18–30 months; auction close precedes deployment by the build-out rules, not by press expectations.
- Marketing names ("5G-Advanced", "6G-ready") are not release features; map them to work items.
- Spectrum positions in filings are advocacy; treat as [Stated] with an incentive note.
