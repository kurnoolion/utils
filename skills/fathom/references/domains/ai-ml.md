# Domain pack — AI/ML research, models, adoption

## Primary sources (decide)
- Papers with code and evals (arXiv plus the repo), model and system cards, official lab blogs and release notes, API docs and pricing pages (use the Wayback Machine for price history).
- Benchmarks and leaderboards: the benchmark's own site and paper; Epoch AI for compute and cost estimates; Stanford AI Index for annual aggregates.
- Money and capacity: hyperscaler and lab-parent earnings transcripts (capex guidance, AI revenue run-rates), chip vendor roadmaps, data-centre power filings.
- Regulation: EU AI Act obligation dates, US federal and state acts, standards bodies (NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001).
- Deployed usage: company disclosures of seats, revenue or headcount effects; surveys with published methodology only.

## Secondary (frame, not decide)
SemiAnalysis, The Information, Stratechery, Import AI, Interconnects, Latent Space, reputable newsletters; analyst reports (Gartner, IDC) with methodology caveats.

## Expert vocabulary for queries
Pretraining / post-training, RLHF / RLVR, test-time compute, mixture of experts, distillation, quantization, context window, tool use and agents, MCP, evals and pass@k, contamination, scaling laws, tokens per second, cost per million tokens, GPU-hours, cluster size, capex intensity.

## Signpost calendar
- Model releases: frontier labs ship on roughly quarterly cadences; watch system cards, not keynotes.
- Hyperscaler earnings (late Jan / Apr / Jul / Oct): capex guidance is the hardest money signal.
- Chip roadmaps: vendor developer conferences (spring) and earnings.
- Regulation: EU AI Act GPAI and high-risk obligation dates; US state-law effective dates.
- Benchmarks: a threshold crossed by two independent labs is a signal; one lab's self-report is a claim.

## Pitfalls specific to this domain
- Self-reported evals and benchmark contamination: prefer third-party or held-out evaluations.
- Capability (papers, demos) is not deployment (revenue, workflows, headcount). Narrative runs 1–3 years ahead of usage.
- "AGI" and "agent" have no agreed definition; write the definition into the resolvable question.
- Run-rate revenue is not recognized revenue; seats are not usage.
- Lab statements move markets, regulators and hiring; tag them [Stated] with the incentive.
